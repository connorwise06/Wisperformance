Privacy policy.

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy explains what information Wise Performance ("I", "me") collects through wiseperformancecoach.com and how it's used. Questions? Email connor@wiseperformancecoach.com.

What I collect

  • Contact form: your name, email, phone number (optional), the plan you're interested in, and your training goals.
  • Free program signup: your email address, collected through Kit.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. I never see or store your full card details.
  • Consult bookings: handled by Calendly, which collects the details you enter when booking.
  • Site analytics: Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymous usage data, like which pages are visited and what device you're on.

How I use it

  • To reply to your messages and set up your coaching.
  • To deliver your program and communicate with you through the Relay app.
  • To send the free program and occasional emails you signed up for. You can unsubscribe anytime from any email.
  • To understand how the site is used so I can improve it.

I don't sell your personal information.

Services I use

Your information may be processed by the tools that run this site and my coaching: Formspree (contact form), Kit (email), Stripe (payments), Calendly (scheduling), Google Analytics (site analytics), Relay (coaching app), TrainHeroic (training teams), Fourthwall (digital products), and GitHub Pages (website hosting). Each has its own privacy policy.

Athletes under 18

This site isn't directed at children under 13. If you're under 18, please have a parent or guardian contact me or sign up on your behalf.

Your choices

You can ask me to see, correct, or delete the information I have about you by emailing connor@wiseperformancecoach.com. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top of this page.
