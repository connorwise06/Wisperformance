FAQ
Questions?
Answered.

Everything you need to know before you start. Still have a question? Book a free consult.

How does online coaching work?

After a quick consult call, I build your program and deliver it through the Relay app within 48 hours. You log your workouts in the app, send me videos of your lifts, and message me directly whenever you need to.

What's the difference between Essential and Elite?

Essential gets you a custom monthly training plan, direct messaging, and the WP exercise library. Elite adds weekly programming adjustments, unlimited video form reviews, priority messaging, and performance testing and tracking.

Do I need a full gym?

No. Your program is built around the equipment you actually have access to. We'll go over your setup on the consult call.

Who do you coach?

Athletes from youth to college level, across football, basketball, softball, soccer, and more. No matter your background or experience level, the program is built for where you are right now.

How do video form reviews work?

Film your set on your phone and send it through Relay. I'll send back specific feedback on exactly what to fix, not generic cues.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Coaching is billed month to month, and you can cancel anytime on your own. You can also message me and I'll take care of it for you.

Is the consult call really free?

Yes. It's a free call to talk through your goals and current training and find the right plan for you. No pressure, no pitch.
